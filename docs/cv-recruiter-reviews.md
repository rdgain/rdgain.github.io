# Recruiter reviews of the tailored CVs

**Review date:** 19 November 2026

These are content reviews from the perspective of recruiters for the four
retained CVs. The dedicated Procedural Content Generation CV and its index
card were removed at the user's request. The reviews assess relevance,
evidence, clarity and likely follow-up questions based on the CVs and
supporting project links. They do not independently verify every claim.

## AI, Machine Learning & LLM

**CV:** [AI, Machine Learning & LLM](../cv/ai-ml-llm.md)

**Recruiter read:** The CV now targets applied AI prototyping and evaluation,
not research positions or production AI-product roles. It is a credible
profile for teams that need AI experimentation, technical prototypes or
evaluation tooling.

**What works**

- The LLM evaluation prototype leads with specific scope: 11 models, 29
  tasks, Python/Java integration and an interface for experiments.
- The RPG application demonstrates independent hands-on prototyping with
  TypeScript, React and LLM-powered chat.
- The Tabletop R&D description distinguishes client research work and
  simulation scale from standalone AI products; this is important for setting
  accurate expectations.
- The research/publications archive remains available as supporting context
  without turning the CV into an academic publication list.

**Likely questions or gaps**

- The work is primarily prototypes, evaluation systems and research
  capabilities; the CV does not claim deployed AI products. It should not be
  used unchanged for roles that require prior production AI product ownership.
- Recruiters may ask which LLM APIs or models were integrated, what the
  evaluation measured, and what technical choices were made in the RPG chat.
- The prototype projects demonstrate breadth, but the CV has limited
  production software evidence beyond client game projects at Tabletop R&D.

**Highest-value improvement**

For prototype-focused applied AI roles, add verified model/API, evaluation
and implementation details where available. Keep the explicit prototype and
research-tool framing; do not imply that Tabletop R&D's AI tooling was
deployed as a product.

## Simulation & Game Development

**CV:** [Simulation & Game Development](../cv/simulation-game-development.md)

**Recruiter read:** The revised ordering and headline are much more
studio-facing: a Unity game, strategy environment and detective game lead,
followed by frameworks as evidence of game design, development and
programming.

**What works**

- Rebirth leads with team game design and engineering, followed by project
  work on Tribes and City of Kalux; the arrangement makes the game-development
  focus clear before introducing research frameworks.
- Rebirth and Tribes include source-code links, giving recruiters a direct
  route to inspect implementations.
- TAG, GVGAI and Pommerman are grouped as examples of framework, competition
  and game-environment programming rather than presented as the main target.
- Omitting publications keeps the document focused on technical game roles.

**Likely questions or gaps**

- The CV has one Unity game and two more design/environment examples, but
  still lacks an immediately playable build or gameplay footage.
- Rebirth began as a 2016 student/team project; studio recruiters may ask
  which systems were implemented recently and which were part of the original
  team work.
- The project descriptions do not yet show runtime constraints, testing,
  production pipeline or player-facing outcomes.

**Highest-value improvement**

For studio roles, add a playable build or short gameplay demo if available.
State the personal gameplay/system contributions to Rebirth, Tribes and City
of Kalux precisely, separating the 2016 team work from the later Unity 6
modernization.

## Software Engineering

**CV:** [Software Engineering](../cv/software-engineering.md)

**Recruiter read:** A credible engineering profile for game, simulation or
research-software teams. For general software engineering positions, the
evidence currently leans toward specialized frameworks and independent
projects rather than commercial software delivery.

**What works**

- The skill groups make the broad stack easy to scan, and the profile offers
  a clear architecture-and-systems narrative.
- TAG's 60+ games and code-review/coordination experience demonstrate
  long-lived framework work and collaboration at meaningful scope.
- The TypeScript/React RPG application and website redesign show
  application-level and web experience alongside Java and game systems.
- Tabletop R&D adds technical direction and client-facing research
  prototyping, with the AI tools explicitly described as not deployed
  standalone products.

**Likely questions or gaps**

- The CV does not state concrete delivery outcomes for the software projects:
  users, releases, operational responsibilities, performance, reliability or
  adoption are not described.
- The project sequence starts with TAG and GVGAI. That is persuasive for
  game/research engineering but less immediately relevant to a web or
  full-stack recruiter than the TypeScript/React application.
- Conventional automated testing, CI/CD, API ownership and production support
  are not evidenced. The CV does list simulation and evaluation; avoid implying
  a separate software QA practice without specific examples.

**Highest-value improvement**

For general full-stack applications, lead with the RPG creator and website
work, followed by Tabletop R&D research tooling; keep TAG prominent for
framework or game-software roles. Add verified details about architecture,
interfaces and delivery outcomes, and include a live demo or code link where
appropriate. Do not add testing or production claims without evidence.

## Research & Data Science

**CV:** [Research & Data Science](../cv/research-data-science.md)

**Recruiter read:** Strong for Game AI research and research-oriented data
analysis. It is not yet a broad data-science CV for roles expecting production
data pipelines or business analytics.

**What works**

- The PhD, research methods, algorithms and selected projects form a coherent
  research profile.
- The BoardGameGeek analysis gives a quantified finding (28% and 32% of
  rating variance) rather than relying only on general method descriptions.
- Gameplay-trace analysis and RHEA demonstrate complementary research
  questions and methods; the selected publications and archive link support
  further evaluation.
- Supervision shows research leadership. Tabletop R&D is now separate under
  applied data science and makes clear that client game projects can progress
  into production while the AI tools remain research prototypes.

**Likely questions or gaps**

- A general data-science recruiter may look for larger non-game datasets,
  production data workflows, dashboards or business outcomes. The CV mainly
  evidences academic game data and simulation.
- The publication list now includes the RHEA paper's IEEE Transactions on
  Games (ToG) venue and full volume/page details; author position may still be
  useful for academic applications.
- The 28%/32% metric would be stronger with a brief explanation of the
  evaluation target or dataset, provided that can be stated accurately.

**Highest-value improvement**

Keep this version for Game AI, academic research and research-data roles.
For each application, distinguish academic research from applied data
science, and include authorship position where useful. For general
data-science roles, create a separate variant only if additional verified
evidence exists for production data workflows or non-game analytics.

## Overall assessment

The four retained pages are concise, visually consistent and role-specific
starting points. The AI and game-development CVs now have clearer applied and
studio targets, respectively; the research CV distinguishes applied client
data work from academic research. The main opportunity is to add demonstrable
outcomes or work samples where available, without presenting research
prototypes as deployed AI products. Recommendations are application-dependent
and do not imply that unsupported claims should be added.
